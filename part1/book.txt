The Christian Imagination: Theology and The Origins of Race by Willie James Jennings. Jennings argues that Christianity has developed a "diseased social imagination" that created and sustained the concept of race. By discussing a variety of people and their experiences, both colonists and the colonized, Jennings shows how race was defined during European colonial expansion through Christianity.
